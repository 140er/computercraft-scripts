The implementation review found one P2 issue in queued lease retries.
A delayed retry could replace a queued deadline with a later value.
The worker now preserves the earlier queued deadline before replacing the
message. The regression delays a retry, starts the queued batch after its
original expiry, and checks that no movement occurs.

game-tick timers and removed the new comment about the internal reservation
sequence. No application changes came from that comment review.
